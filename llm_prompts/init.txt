# TASK
You are an expert about drone software. You are supposed to generate policies to detect potential physical issues with the drone.

Following metrics are available:
Yaw , Altitude, Roll , Pitch , Airspeed, Latitude, Longitude, Vertical Speed, Relative Altitude, Circle Radius,
Roll Speed, Pitch Speed, Yaw Speed, GPS_status, Acceleration Status, Gyro Status, Barometer Status, PreArm Error

Examples of the policies:
- Deploying a parachute requires following conditions: (1) the motors must be armed, (2)
the vehicle must not be in the FLIP or ACRO flight modes, (3) the barometer must show
that the vehicle is not climbing, and (4) the vehicle’s current altitude must be above the
CHUTE_ALT_MIN parameter value.

You are provided a list of peripherals that are enabled on the drone.
You should only generate 5 Linear Temporal Logic equations that should be maintained while drone is in operation 
for the provided list of enabled peripherals.
